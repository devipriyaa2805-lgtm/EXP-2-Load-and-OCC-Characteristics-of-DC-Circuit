
# EXPERIMENT NO: 2

# **OCC AND LOAD CHARACTERISTICS OF DC SHUNT GENERATOR**

## AIM

To conduct the open-circuit and load tests on a D.C. shunt generator and obtain its characteristics.

## APPARATUS REQUIRED

| Sl. No. | Apparatus | Range | Type | Quantity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Voltmeter | 1–230 V | As shown in simulation | 1 |
| 2 | Ammeter | 1–20 A | As shown in simulation | 1 |
| 3 | Ammeter | 0–20 A | As shown in simulation | 1 |
| 4 | Rheostat | As required | Wire-wound | 1 |
| 5 | Connecting wires | — | — | As required |

## FUSE RATING CALCULATION

### No-Load Test
Fuse rating = 10% of the rated full-load current.

### Load Test
Fuse rating = 125% of the rated full-load current.

## PRECAUTIONS

1. Keep the motor-side field rheostat at minimum resistance.
2. Keep the generator-side field rheostat at maximum resistance initially.
3. Ensure the starter is in the OFF position before switching on the supply.
4. Keep the DPST switches open before starting the experiment.

## PROCEDURE FOR OPEN-CIRCUIT TEST

1. Make the connections according to the circuit diagram.
2. Start the motor using the three-point starter.
3. Adjust the motor speed to the rated speed using the motor field rheostat while the generator is disconnected from the load.
4. Gradually vary the generator field rheostat and record the open-circuit voltage and corresponding field current.
5. Continue the readings up to 125% of the rated generator voltage, as instructed.
6. Switch off the motor after returning the rheostats to their initial positions.

## PROCEDURE FOR LOAD TEST

1. Make the connections according to the circuit diagram.
2. Start the prime mover using the three-point starter and bring it to rated speed.
3. Adjust the generator field rheostat to obtain the rated voltage.
4. Record the ammeter and voltmeter readings at no load.
5. Connect the load and record readings for different load currents up to the rated current.
6. Return the load and rheostats to their initial positions after completing the observations.
7. Switch off the motor.

## CALCULATION

### 1. Open-Circuit Test

The open-circuit voltage increases with the field current.

From the observation table:

- When current = 0.16 A, voltage = 115 V.
- When current = 0.20 A, voltage = 126 V.
- When current = 0.30 A, voltage = 142 V.

### 2. Load Test

The voltage drop is calculated using:

Voltage Drop = No-load voltage - Load voltage

Taking the first recorded voltage as the reference:

No-load reference voltage = 220 V

**Calculation 1:**

At load current = 2.8 A,

Voltage Drop = 220 - 212

Voltage Drop = 8 V

**Calculation 2:**

At load current = 7 A,

Voltage Drop = 220 - 195

Voltage Drop = 25 V

**Calculation 3:**

At load current = 12.7 A,

Voltage Drop = 220 - 176

Voltage Drop = 44 V

### 3. Percentage Voltage Drop

Percentage Voltage Drop = (Voltage Drop / Reference Voltage) × 100

= (44 / 220) × 100

= 20%
